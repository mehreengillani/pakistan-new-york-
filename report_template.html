Four south Brooklyn areas (Borough Park, Coney Island, Flatbush and Midwood, and Sheepshead Bay) hold about people (±), or of the city total.

These are survey estimates, so each comes with a margin of error. The coefficient of variation (CV) divides the standard error by the estimate. Under 30% is reasonably precise. At 30% or more, or when the estimate is zero, it is weak. In areas the margin of error is as large as the estimate itself.

What the data can and cannot say

The two datasets sit side by side in this report. Nothing in them shows that the Pakistani community in New York drives the trade that enters through the port. Trade data records where goods came into the US. Community data counts residents. A test of any link needs importer or distributor records.

Trade data

Customs district means port of entry, not final buyer. Goods can enter in New York and be sold anywhere. Figures are goods only and exclude services. Dollar values are nominal, in current dollars with no adjustment for inflation, so growth rates overstate real growth. The causes of the 2021–2022 jump and the 2023 fall are not analyzed. Tariff changes were not analyzed either.

Community data

"Pakistani alone" excludes people who report more than one race or origin, so it is a floor for the community. Small-area estimates carry large margins of error. Ranks between close neighbors are approximate.

Method and sources

Everything on this page comes from one Python script (analysis.py) that reads the downloaded Census files, runs the checks below, and writes the data this page reads.

  1. Load and check. Districts must add up to the API's own total row, there must be no duplicate rows, and the New York file must match the New York rows of the all-districts file. An early version of the summary added the API's "TOTAL FOR ALL DISTRICTS" rows to the district rows and doubled every total. The script now stops if districts and totals disagree.
  2. Trade. Imports for consumption, summed by year and district. Product groups use HS 2-digit chapters. Growth uses the compound annual rate so the 2021–2022 jump does not distort the trend.
  3. New York. Share of the national total, rank among districts, an entry-port concentration index, and a location quotient for each product group.
  4. Community. City and borough totals add county estimates, with margins of error combined as the square root of the sum of squares. Each area gets a coefficient of variation from its margin of error divided by 1.645.
Imports for consumption
Goods that enter the US market, excluding goods held in bonded storage or re-exported.
HS chapter
The first two digits of the Harmonized System product code.
PUMA
Public Use Microdata Area, a Census area of about 100,000 people. NYC has 55.
Margin of error
The range around a survey estimate at 90% confidence.
Location quotient
New York's share of a product divided by its share of all imports. Above 1 means over-represented.

Sources: Census International Trade API, American Community Survey 5-year data.
