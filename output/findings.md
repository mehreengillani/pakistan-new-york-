# Findings

## US imports from Pakistan
- Imports for consumption rose from $3.49bn in 2010 to $5.47bn in 2025 (+57%, 3.0% a year).
- The peak was $5.98bn in 2022; the next year it fell 18%.
- Apparel and home textiles were 73% of 2025 imports.
- Apparel (woven) grew fastest of the groups above $1bn (+164% since 2010).

## New York
- New York City district took $1.54bn in 2025, a 28% share; it ranked first among US customs districts in every year of the data.
- Its share ranged from 24% (2020) to 32% (2010). New York's imports grew +36% against +57% nationally, so its share was 28% in 2025.
- Next largest: Savannah, GA (13%) and Los Angeles, CA (12%).
- New York over-indexes on Rice & cereals (64% of the national total, location quotient 2.3) and Apparel (woven) (44%, LQ 1.6).
- Entry-point concentration (HHI) went from 1685 to 1323.

## Pakistani New Yorkers (ACS 2020-2024)
- About 49,138 residents (±3,593) identify as Pakistani alone, 0.58% of the city.
- About 34,160 (±2,426) were born in Pakistan.
- Four south Brooklyn areas hold about 13,081 (±1,938), or 27% of the city total.
- 37 of 55 neighborhood areas are weak estimates: 35 have a coefficient of variation of 30% or more and 2 have an estimate of zero. In 10 the margin of error is as large as the estimate.

## Limits
- Customs district = where goods entered the US, not who bought them.
- Dollar values are nominal (current dollars, not adjusted for inflation), so growth rates overstate real growth.
- "Pakistani alone" excludes multiracial residents, so it is a floor for the community.
- Neighborhood ranks between close areas are within the margin of error.
